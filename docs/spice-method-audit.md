# Complete spice-method audit

Checked 30 October 2026 against all **73** current recipes. Every recipe appears exactly once below. This pass adds **25 specific powder-method updates**, in addition to the **7 publisher powder routes** already selected in the first release. There is no seven-recipe cap. 47 recipe records advance from their previously published version in this release, including scaling/measure corrections; that count is not the same as the method-update count. Existing cooking sessions retain their original versioned snapshots.

These primary dispositions are exclusive. Retained-preparation flags in individual rows can overlap: a converted recipe can still contain fresh coconut, a fermented batter or whole popu. An exception is not labeled already powder-compatible. This audit does **not** claim that all recipes are powder-only or that every whole ingredient can be replaced without changing the dish.

Ready individual-spice adaptations use [McCormick Kitchens’ approximate grinding yields](https://www.mccormick.com/blogs/how-to/how-to-grind-your-own-spices-plus-spice-conversions-for-your-favorite-seasonings) and the [Spiceitupp author’s ground-fenugreek guidance](https://spiceitupp.com/substitute-for-fenugreek-seeds/), with additional recipe-specific references where needed. These support labeled household adaptations, not identical flavour or potency across brands. Powders go into an appropriate moist base rather than being dry-roasted like whole seeds. The uncooked pungent-mustard chutney role is specifically retained.

Volume-to-volume conversions are explicit; published mass measures such as pure mustard or comparable-heat pure dried-chilli powder remain weighed measures. No mass-to-teaspoon density is invented. Tiny published pinches remain pinches when no defensible spoon quantity exists. Prepared mustard condiments and mixed commercial masalas are not treated as pure individual spices. Fresh vegetable/nut/leaf bases, rice/urad batters, fermented-batter fenugreek, intentional whole popu and rice aromatics retain their stated source functions. Specific dried-chilli exceptions are named below. All recipe methods remain **not independently kitchen-tested**.
